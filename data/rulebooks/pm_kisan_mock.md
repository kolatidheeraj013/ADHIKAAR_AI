# PM-Kisan (MOCK) rulebook

**MOCK guidance only — not an official government rulebook.** This document
contains synthetic demo text for ADHIKAAR retrieval and explanation citations.

## Who this MOCK scheme is for

This MOCK scheme is for a farmer seeking support for individually held
cultivable agricultural land. A farmer asking about farming, land ownership,
crop support, or PM-Kisan should be matched to this scheme.

## Required documents (MOCK)

Provide an Aadhaar document, a land record, and a bank passbook. A clear photo
of each document is needed for this demo. The rules engine, rather than this
text, checks whether the documents and extracted facts satisfy the scheme.

## Eligibility guidance (MOCK)

The simplified demo rules require land ownership greater than zero hectares,
an individual (not institutional) land holder, and a declaration that nobody
in the family pays income tax. The benefit shown by this demo is INR 6,000 per
year (MOCK benefit).
