# State Scholarship (MOCK) rulebook

**MOCK guidance only — not an official government rulebook.** This document
contains synthetic demo text for ADHIKAAR retrieval and explanation citations.

## Who this MOCK scheme is for

This MOCK scheme is for a student seeking an education scholarship or school
support. A student asking about studies, tuition, academic marks, education
funding, or a scholarship should be matched to this scheme.

## Required documents (MOCK)

Provide an Aadhaar document, an income certificate, a marksheet, and a bonafide
certificate. A clear photo of each document is needed for this demo. The rules
engine, rather than this text, checks whether the documents and extracted facts
satisfy the scheme.

## Eligibility guidance (MOCK)

The simplified demo rules require annual family income of no more than INR
250,000, last examination marks of at least 75 percent, and a declaration that
the educational institution is recognized. This is an education scholarship
(MOCK benefit).
